# 64 — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 2026*

64 is a chess app for iPhone and iPad. It is built to keep what you do on your device.

## What stays on your device

Your games, puzzle progress, settings and game history are stored only on your iPhone or
iPad. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

## No account, no ads, no tracking

64 has no sign-up and no account. It shows no ads and does not track you across other
apps or websites.

## Purchases

64 Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your
payment details. The app only asks Apple whether 64 Pro is active on your Apple ID.

## Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which parts of the app are used, 64 sends a small number of anonymous signals,
such as "a game was finished", "a puzzle was solved" or "the Pro page was opened", via
[TelemetryDeck](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/), a privacy-focused analytics service
based in Germany. These signals contain no name, email, contacts, location or other
personal data, and the identifier they carry is a random value that is anonymised before
it leaves your device. They cannot be used to identify you.

## Children

64 does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.
