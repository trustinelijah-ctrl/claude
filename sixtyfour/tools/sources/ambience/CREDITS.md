# Ambience credits

All scenes come from the Red Library sound-effects collection (USC Cinema / Sunset
Editorial), published on the Internet Archive and dedicated to the public domain under
CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- Rain: "R30-24 Rain on Roof and Concrete", https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Nature_Rain
- Storm: "R10-04 Heavy Rain and Wind", https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Nature_Rain
- Fireplace: "R09-68 Slightly Muffled Fire", https://archive.org/details/Red_Library_Fire

`tools/scenes.py` downloads them into this folder and builds the loops.
