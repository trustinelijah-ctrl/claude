Nothing. 64 contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not use any third-party services that collect data.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Because the data exists only on your device, it is kept until you delete it. Deleting 64 from your device deletes all of it. There is nothing for us to delete on our side, since we hold nothing about you. If you write to us by email, we keep that correspondence only as long as needed to answer you.
