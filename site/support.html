Restoring 64 Pro

On a new iPhone or iPad, open 64, go to Options ▸ More, and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Cancelling a subscription

Open Options ▸ More ▸ Manage subscription in 64, or the Settings app ▸ your name ▸ Subscriptions ▸ 64. Cancel at least a day before the renewal or the end of the free week.

Refunds

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

No sound?

Check Options ▸ Sound: Pieces, clock and board should be on and the volume up. If you want 64 to stay quiet with the silent switch on, turn off Play in silent mode.

Family Sharing

64 Pro can be shared with up to five family members through Apple's Family Sharing.
